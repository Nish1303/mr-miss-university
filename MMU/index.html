About The Pageant

More than a pageant —
a transformative journey.

Mr. and Miss University Sri Lanka is more than a pageant — it's a transformative journey. We empower university students across the nation to discover their true potential, build unwavering confidence, refine their personality, and embrace excellence.

Through talent showcases, professional grooming sessions, and leadership training, we shape the next generation of Sri Lankan icons.
